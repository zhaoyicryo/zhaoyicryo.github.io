From June 28 to July 2, 2026, Yi Zhao attended the **"3rd ACOP 2026 conference"** held in Ulaanbaatar, Mongolia.

ACOP 2026, under the theme **"Challenges Posed by Asian Permafrost Thaw to the Global Community"**, offers a vital platform for students, researchers, scientists, engineers, and policymakers to share knowledge, build partnerships, and further advance our understanding of permafrost and cold region environments. ACOP 2026 brought together more than 360 scientists and young researchers from 25 countries, including Canada, China, Germany, Japan, Russia, and the United States. It served as an important platform for sharing the latest advances in permafrost science and cold-region environments, with discussions covering permafrost dynamics, climate-permafrost-ecosystem interactions, infrastructure resilience, carbon feedbacks, and sustainable development in cold regions.

At the session **"Modeling Permafrost Dynamics Across Scales"**, Yi Zhao gave an oral presentation entitled **"A Hybrid Modeling Approach for Improved Simulation of Thermal-Hydrological Dynamics in the Active Layer on the Qinghai-Tibet Plateau."** He shared recent progress on permafrost modeling in the Qinghai-Tibet Plateau, the development of key thermal and hydrological datasets, and new findings on near-surface ground ice changes under a warming and wetter climate. He also had discussions with international colleagues on permafrost modeling techniques and permafrost degradation processes across the Qinghai-Tibet Plateau.

During the visit, Yi Zhao also enjoyed local food and explored the beautiful cityscape of Ulaanbaatar. A really enjoyable experience!!
